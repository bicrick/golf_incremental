# v2 Vision

## Elevator pitch

Run a **cracked, lonely driving range** as a range rat: hit buckets of balls with satisfying contact, walk the fairway collecting litter before it piles up, hire oddball help, and turn dirt into the coziest range on the hill — while sweet-spot upgrades eventually send perfect shots into the absurd.

## Creative north star

**Fortune Mill breadth. Ghibli warmth. Superintendent heart.**

| Pillar | v2 meaning |
|--------|------------|
| **Superintendent fantasy** | You improve the *place*, not just your stats. Cracks → grass → bays → lights. |
| **Bucket compulsion** | Small burst of swings → mandatory harvest → spend → repeat. "One more bucket." |
| **Contact, not charge** | Skill = timing the downswing contact (rat anim frame 9), not holding a power bar. |
| **Looks before yards** | Early hits **fly** to a readable zone; payout can stay small. |
| **Calm default** | Pastoral range, pickup plinks, gnome wave — not slot-machine baseline. |
| **Jackpot spikes** | Bullseyes, bucket combo clears, ridiculous perfect carry — brief spikes, then calm. |
| **Down-the-line truth** | Camera fixed over shoulder; balls land in a **depth cluster**, not a spread field. |

## What we are not

- Hold-to-charge power bar as the primary fantasy (v1 mechanic — retired in v2)
- Early visual dinks that teach "this game isn't about hitting"
- Pickup as a boring menu or long unskippable chore
- Gophers that steal currency already earned
- Purple-heavy UI or neon casino aesthetic

## Player identity

**Range rat superintendent** — you hit when the bucket allows, you pick up when it's empty, you spend on the range and your clubs. Later you hire crew and unlock new range zones (target bay, night range).

## Inspiration (unchanged engine)

Godot 4 + GDScript, parallax 2.5D, pixel art. See [../technical/00-stack.md](../technical/00-stack.md).
